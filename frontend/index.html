Research question

Can an AI lab design a cheap coating of five layers or fewer that beats Stanford's seven-layer cooler, using fewer simulations than standard search?

The bottleneck. Every candidate coating costs one physics simulation. Random search needs 184 on average to hit the target; the best standard optimiser needs 37.5. The lab wins only if it needs fewer.
